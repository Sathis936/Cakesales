🥐 Course Details

French Pastry Essentials

Master the art of classic French pastry with Chef Antoine Laurent — a 6-week intensive program for aspiring bakers and pastry enthusiasts.

🍰

About This Course

Immerse yourself in the exquisite world of French patisserie with our signature French Pastry Essentials program. Over six transformative weeks, you'll learn time-honored techniques passed down through generations of Parisian pastry chefs.

From the delicate lamination of buttery croissants to the airy perfection of macaron shells, every lesson builds your confidence and skill. You'll work hands-on in our professional kitchen with premium imported ingredients, guided by Chef Antoine Laurent, a former executive pastry chef at Michelin-starred restaurants.

What You'll Learn

  • Classic puff pastry and croissant lamination techniques
  • Perfect choux pastry for eclairs and profiteroles
  • French tart making with pate sucree and pate brisee
  • Macaron mastery — shells, fillings, and flavor pairing
  • Diplomat, pastry cream, and mousseline fillings
  • Professional plating and decorative techniques
  • Kitchen efficiency, timing, and production workflow
  • Ingredient sourcing and quality assessment

Week 1: Foundations & Puff Pastry

6 Lessons
  • Introduction to French pastry ingredients & tools
  • Butter block preparation & lamination theory
  • Classic croissant shaping & proofing
  • Pain au chocolat & almond croissant
  • Troubleshooting lamination issues

Week 2: Choux & Eclairs

5 Lessons
  • Perfect choux pastry — panade technique
  • Piping techniques for eclairs & profiteroles
  • Pastry cream & diplomat cream variations
  • Fondant glazes & chocolate decor
  • Classic Paris-Brest creation

Week 3-4: Tarts & Short Pastries

8 Lessons
  • Pate sucree, pate brisee, and pate sablee
  • Blind baking & tart shell perfection
  • Fruit tarts — seasonal composition
  • Chocolate ganache & salted caramel tarts
  • Lemon meringue & creme brulee tarts

Week 5: Macaron Mastery

5 Lessons
  • French vs Italian meringue methods
  • Macaronage — the perfect consistency
  • Resting, skin formation & baking
  • Feet development & troubleshooting
  • Buttercream, ganache & curd fillings

Week 6: Final Project & Showcase

4 Lessons
  • Design your signature pastry collection
  • Production scheduling & time management
  • Professional plating & photography
  • Peer showcase & graduation ceremony

Chef Antoine Laurent

Executive Pastry Chef & Lead Instructor

Biography

With over 22 years of experience in the world of haute patisserie, Chef Antoine brings unparalleled expertise to SweetCraft Studio. After training at the prestigious Ferrandi School in Paris, he honed his craft at three-Michelin-starred establishments including Alain Ducasse and Pierre Gagnaire.

As a former World Pastry Cup jury member and author of three bestselling cookbooks, Chef Antoine is passionate about preserving traditional techniques while encouraging creative innovation among his students.

Credentials & Awards

  • Meilleur Ouvrier de France (MOF) — Pastry Division, 2015
  • Gold Medal — Coupe du Monde de la Patisserie, 2011
  • Michelin Star Experience — 15 years across 3-star kitchens
  • Published Author — "L'Art de la Patisserie" & 2 more titles

Do I need prior baking experience to join this course?
No prior experience is required! While some students do come with basic skills, our curriculum is designed to take you from complete beginner to confident pastry maker. Chef Antoine and our assistant instructors provide personalized guidance at every step. We do recommend practicing basic home baking (cookies, simple cakes) a few weeks before starting to get comfortable with kitchen basics.
What's included in the course fee? Are there any extra costs?
Everything is included in your tuition: all premium ingredients (French butter, Belgian chocolate, fresh fruits), professional tool kit (pastry bag, tips, bench scraper, and more for you to keep), printed recipe cards, chef's apron, daily coffee and snacks, course certificate, and a professional photo shoot of your final creations. There are absolutely no hidden fees or extra costs.
How large are the classes? Will I get individual attention?
We strictly limit our class size to 6 students maximum. This is our promise to you — no crowded kitchens, no missed demonstrations, no rushing. With Chef Antoine leading plus one assistant instructor, the student-to-chef ratio is just 3:1 during practical sessions. Every single student works at their own fully equipped station.
What is the schedule like? Can I work full-time while studying?
We offer two tracks: (1) Weekday Intensive — Mon/Wed/Fri, 9AM to 2PM over 6 weeks, and (2) Weekend Flexible — Sat 10AM to 4PM and Sun 10AM to 2PM over 6 weeks. Many of our students do hold full-time jobs and choose the weekend option. You can see all available batches on our Schedule page.
Do you offer payment plans or financial assistance?
Yes! We offer flexible 0% interest payment plans split into 3, 6, or 12 monthly installments with no credit check required. We also partner with two culinary scholarship programs for eligible students. Additionally, military veterans, hospitality workers, and repeat students receive automatic 15% discounts. Please contact our admissions team for details.
