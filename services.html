Frequently Asked Questions ❓

Got Baking Questions? We've Got Answers

Everything you need to know before joining our courses. Still unsure? Reach out — our team loves talking pastry!

I'm a complete beginner who's never baked before. Which course should I start with?

Great question! We'd absolutely recommend starting with our Intro to Baking Fundamentals course. It's designed specifically for total beginners with zero experience. We start from absolute basics — how to measure correctly, read a recipe, use oven settings, and work with common ingredients. The atmosphere is supportive, no silly questions allowed! After that, most students move into Artisan Bread Making or Cookies & Brownies Bootcamp to build confidence with fun, rewarding recipes.

Do I need to bring my own ingredients or tools to class?

Nope! We provide absolutely everything — all professional-grade ingredients (the same premium imported brands our chefs use professionally), all equipment, mixing bowls, tools, aprons, and even recipe cards you can take home. You just need to show up ready to learn (and ready to eat your delicious creations at the end of each class!). For longer programs, we do provide a list of recommended home tools if you'd like to practice between classes, but that's completely optional.

What's the class size and will I get individual attention?

We intentionally keep our classes very small — max 8-12 students per class depending on the program (Professional courses are capped at just 4-6 students for ultra-personalized training). This ensures every student gets plenty of one-on-one time with the chef. Our instructors walk around constantly during hands-on sessions to correct technique, answer questions, and make sure nobody gets stuck. It's basically private training at group prices!

Do you offer payment plans or scholarships for longer programs?

Absolutely! We believe financial circumstances shouldn't stand in the way of a great baking education. For our Professional Pastry Diploma and other longer programs, we offer 0% interest payment plans spread over 6 to 12 months with no credit check required. We also have a limited number of partial scholarships each quarter for passionate bakers who demonstrate financial need or exceptional creativity. Email us at hello@sweetcraft.com to learn more — we'd love to help you find a way in!

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions like gluten-free or vegan baking?

Yes, absolutely — and our dietary-option recipes are genuinely delicious, not afterthoughts! We can accommodate gluten-free, dairy-free, vegan, nut-free, and kosher/halal diets in nearly all of our courses. Just let us know your dietary needs when you enroll (at least 5 days before class starts so our chefs can prep correctly). We even offer a dedicated 4-week Gluten-Free & Vegan Baking Intensive if that's your main focus. Delicious baked goods are for everyone!

Will I get a certificate after completing a course? Does it help with jobs?

Yes! Every student who completes a course receives a SweetCraft Certificate of Completion signed by your instructor. Our Professional Pastry Diploma program awards a more comprehensive industry-recognized certification that includes skills benchmarks verified by chef assessors. We also have an active job placement network — many bakeries, hotels, and restaurants specifically reach out to SweetCraft when hiring because they trust our training quality. Our diploma graduates boast a 92% job placement rate within 3 months of graduating.
