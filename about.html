🍰 About SweetCraft

Our Story of Passion & Pastry

From a small home kitchen to an internationally acclaimed baking school — discover how SweetCraft became a haven for pastry lovers worldwide.

🌟 Our Journey

Baking Dreams Since 2008

SweetCraft began in a tiny apartment kitchen in Brooklyn, where our founder Marie Laurent spent weekends teaching friends and neighbors the art of French baking. What started as a humble hobby quickly grew into something extraordinary.

Word spread fast about Marie's patient teaching style and incredibly delicious recipes. Within two years, the classes outgrew the apartment, and SweetCraft Baking Studio opened its doors in the heart of the Culinary District.

Today, with over 15 years of excellence, SweetCraft has trained more than 2,500 students from 40+ countries. Our graduates have gone on to open patisseries, work in 5-star hotels, and win prestigious pastry competitions worldwide.

Yet some things never changed — our commitment to quality ingredients, hands-on learning, and the belief that everyone has a pastry chef waiting inside them.

Mission & Vision

Every croissant baked, every cake decorated, every student graduated brings us closer to our vision.

Our Mission

To empower aspiring bakers worldwide with world-class pastry education, preserving traditional techniques while encouraging creative innovation. We strive to make professional baking accessible, enjoyable, and transformative for everyone — regardless of background or experience level.

  • Teach authentic, time-tested recipes
  • Foster creativity and confidence
  • Build a supportive baking community

Our Vision

To become the world's most beloved baking academy — a place where culinary dreams come to life, where tradition meets innovation, and where every student leaves with not just skills, but a lifelong passion for the pastry arts. We envision a world made sweeter, one baker at a time.

  • Global campuses by 2030
  • 100,000+ trained bakers worldwide
  • A pastry academy on every continent
